Why I built this for engineers

In robotics, a model has to hold up in the real world.

I’m Mohammad Khan, a robotics engineer whose work has included autonomous ground vehicles, prosthetics, soft robotics and robotic welding. A weekly fiction-writing practice led me to connect storytelling with engineering decisions.

I call the method Fictioneering: using short fictional scenarios to make assumptions easier to examine. I developed these exercises through sessions at The Sensemakers Club.

Calibration compares a model with reality. These exercises help teams notice what their plans assume about people, systems and time—and decide what needs checking.
